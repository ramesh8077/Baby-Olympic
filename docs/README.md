Baby Olympic Games Kanpur 2026 — Website Content & Design Handoff			
Ready-to-build copy for the WordPress site + campaign landing page + registration form			
			
How this workbook is organised			
Sitemap	The 4 site pages + the standalone campaign landing page, with every section listed in build order.		
Home Page / About / Contact tabs	Section-by-section copy. Columns: Section, Component type, Headline, Sub-head, Body copy, CTA, Design/asset notes. Hand each row to design as one block.		
Campaign Landing Page	A shorter, conversion-focused single page for paid ads (Meta/Google). This is what you point ad traffic to. Fastest thing to launch.		
Registration Form	Field-by-field spec: label, type, required, validation, options, placeholder, help text. This is the build sheet for the form.		
Age Categories & Format	The 4 age bands (2–8 yrs) and the participation format for each.		
Sports & Disciplines	The full sports list, grouped, with age-specific adaptations noted.		
FAQ	Draft questions + answers for the Home page FAQ accordion.		
Sponsors	Tiers, pricing and partner slots pulled from the deck, for the Sponsors section + a 'Become a Sponsor' block.		
SEO & Meta	Page titles, meta descriptions, slug and primary keywords per page.		
Assets Needed	Checklist of images, logos and files design/dev must collect before go-live.		
OPEN ITEMS – Confirm First	Decisions still needed from the organiser (dates, fee, email, domain). These block go-live — clear them first.		
			
EN / HI	English and Hindi copy are both given where the brand uses bilingual lines.
